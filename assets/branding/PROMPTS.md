# Image generation prompts

Method: built-in image_gen; no API/CLI fallback.

## DeX Tablet initial concept (discarded transparent draft)

Use case: logo-brand. Create one polished app icon SYMBOL for DeX Tablet, a wired Galaxy-to-tablet desktop receiver. Transparent background, a single isolated symbol, square 1024x1024 composition. Design a bold, extremely clean geometric landscape tablet outline in luminous mint teal (#60E5CA), with a smaller upright phone overlapping its lower right corner in soft ivory white. A single thick rounded short connector at the bottom visually links the two devices. Big simple shapes and broad consistent strokes, no screen content except one simple short teal connection bar. Flat premium vector-like design, precise rounded corners, front view, no perspective, no shadows, no bloom, no decorative sparks, no text or letters, no Samsung logo, no enclosing tile/background. Keep the entire mark within the central 80% of canvas and centered optically. It must read clearly as connected screens at 24px. This is an actual production logo asset, not a presentation or mockup. Output only one symbol.

Keep the whole symbol compact, centered in the middle 66 percent of the canvas (ample 17 percent margins minimum on every side), large enough for launcher use. No letters or text, no third party logos, no mockup, just one 1024x1024 icon.
